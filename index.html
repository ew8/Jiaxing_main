4. Prevention and Management of Waste Lead-Acid Batteries

4.1 Prevention Mechanisms (Full Life-Cycle Source Control)

①. Source Reduction and Service Life Extension:

  • Optimize routine maintenance: Perform regular balanced charging and internal resistance testing of battery packs to prevent premature failure of the entire battery pack due to degradation of individual cells.
  • Environmental temperature control: Industrial batteries are temperature-sensitive. Ensure that battery rooms or machine rooms provide adequate ventilation and constant temperature conditions (typically maintained at 20°C–25°C) to avoid accelerated battery aging caused by high temperatures.

②. Green Procurement Principles:

  • Prioritize procurement of products that hold green certifications, are easy to recycle, and contain low levels of hazardous impurities such as lead and cadmium.
  • Promote the "trade-in" mechanism by explicitly requiring manufacturers or suppliers in procurement contracts to fulfill reverse logistics recycling obligations.

4.2 Standardized Management (Full-Process Compliance Control):

In accordance with international standards and the "Technical Specification for Pollution Control in the Treatment of Waste Lead-Acid Batteries" (HJ 519-2020) issued by the Ministry of Ecology and Environment, industrial enterprises must strictly comply with the following standards when managing waste batteries:

①. Standardized Storage in Designated Areas within the Plant and Proper Labeling:

  • A dedicated hazardous waste storage room (or dedicated storage facilities) must be established, and hazardous waste identification labels must be prominently displayed.
  • Ground Sealing and Containment: The storage floor must be constructed from impermeable, acid-resistant, and corrosion-resistant materials (e.g., acid-resistant epoxy flooring), and dedicated wastewater/acid containment troughs and collection basins must be installed to strictly prevent leakage that could contaminate soil and groundwater.
  • Structural Integrity Protection: Waste batteries must remain structurally intact. Unauthorized disassembly, crushing, or dumping of waste acid solutions within the plant premises is strictly prohibited.

②. Compliant and Safe Transportation:

  • Leak-proof Packaging: Batteries must be placed upright (with the top facing upward) during transport, using acid-resistant pallets and rainproof canvas to prevent overflow of waste acid solutions due to road vibrations.
  • Qualification Verification: Transportation must be entrusted to vehicles and personnel possessing hazardous goods transport qualifications; such vehicles must be registered with hazardous goods markings.

③. Legally Valid Manifest Handling:

  • Illegal Resale Prohibited: The sale of waste industrial batteries to unqualified "informal operators" or individual vendors is strictly prohibited.
  • Targeted Transfer: Waste batteries must be transferred exclusively to recycled lead enterprises that hold a hazardous waste operation license (with "waste lead-acid batteries" included in their scope of business) or to designated collection points.
  • Transfer Manifest System: In accordance with applicable laws and regulations, accurately complete electronic hazardous waste transfer manifests on the National Solid Waste Management Information System (or the local environmental protection authority platform), ensuring a clear traceability of the flow of each batch of waste.

4.3 Occupational Health and Emergency Prevention

  • Provision of Emergency Supplies: The storage area must be equipped with sufficient adsorbents (e.g., sand or soil) and alkaline neutralizing agents (e.g., quicklime, sodium bicarbonate solution) to enable rapid neutralization in the event of an acid spill.
  • Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) Requirements: Employees who handle or transport used batteries must wear acid-resistant gloves, protective goggles, and acid-resistant work shoes.
  • Fire Safety: The used battery disposal area must be strictly prohibited from open flames and must be equipped with firefighting equipment suitable for both electrical and chemical fires.
